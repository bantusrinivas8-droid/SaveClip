How to use
  1. Copy the link of the video from the app or website.
  2. Paste it above and tap Get video.
  3. Choose MP4 (video) or MP3 (audio) and a quality. The download starts automatically.
📱 Use it from Instagram, YouTube or TikTok on your phone
  1. Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen / Install app. Then in any app tap Share → SaveClip and the link opens here.
  2. iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen. In apps, tap Share → Copy link, open SaveClip and tap 📋 Paste.
Only download videos and music that you own or have permission to use.
Private posts and DRM-protected content are not supported.
